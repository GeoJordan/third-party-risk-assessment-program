# Third-Party Risk Management Methodology

## 1. Purpose
Define the organization's methodology for identifying, assessing, managing, and monitoring risks associated with third-party vendors, suppliers, and service providers.

---

## 2. Scope
Applies to all third parties that:
- Access company systems or networks
- Process, store, or transmit company/customer data
- Support critical business operations
- Provide regulated or outsourced services

---

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities

### Business Owner
- Submit vendor intake requests
- Provide business justification
- Partner in remediation/risk acceptance

### TPRM Analyst
- Conduct vendor intake and tiering
- Coordinate assessments
- Track findings/remediation
- Maintain reporting/dashboarding

### Security/GRC
- Perform technical/security review
- Validate evidence
- Support residual risk determination

### Legal / Privacy / Procurement
- Review contracts, DPAs, and privacy requirements

---

## 4. Vendor Intake Process
1. Business submits vendor request  
2. TPRM performs intake review  
3. Vendor inventory entry created  

---

## 5. Inherent Risk Tiering Methodology

### Risk Factors
- Data Sensitivity
- Access Level
- Business Criticality
- Regulatory Exposure
- Hosting Responsibility

### Tier Thresholds
| Score | Tier |
|-------|------|
| 5–9 | Low |
| 10–14 | Medium |
| 15–19 | High |
| 20–25 | Critical |

---

## 6. Due Diligence Requirements

| Tier | Assessment Requirement |
|------|----------------------|
| Low | Basic Review |
| Medium | Standard Questionnaire |
| High | Enhanced Security Review |
| Critical | Full Assessment + Leadership Approval |

---

## 7. Security Assessment Methodology

### Review Domains
- Access Management
- Encryption
- Logging & Monitoring
- Vulnerability Management
- Incident Response
- Business Continuity / DR
- Compliance Certifications
- Subprocessor Management

---

## 8. Findings Management

### Severity Ratings
| Severity | Description |
|---------|------------|
| Low | Minor control gap |
| Medium | Moderate weakness |
| High | Significant security/compliance risk |
| Critical | Immediate unacceptable risk |

---

## 9. Residual Risk Determination
Residual risk is calculated based on:
- Inherent Risk Tier
- Control Maturity
- Findings Severity
- Compensating Controls

---

## 10. Risk Acceptance Process
High/Critical residual risk vendors require:
- Business Owner Acknowledgement
- Security Review Approval
- Leadership / Risk Committee Sign-Off

---

## 11. Continuous Monitoring
Vendors are reassessed:
- Annually for High/Critical Vendors
- Biennially for Medium Vendors
- As Needed for Low Vendors

Monitoring activities include:
- Reassessment
- Incident Review
- Contract Review
- Security Rating Monitoring

---

## 12. Exceptions
Documented exceptions require:
- Business justification
- Compensating controls
- Defined expiration date
- Leadership approval

---

## 13. Reporting and Metrics
Tracked metrics include:
- Vendor Count by Tier
- Assessment Completion Rate
- Open Findings
- Residual Risk Distribution
- Overdue Reassessments
